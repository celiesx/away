How this works

Your email is only used to sign you in. Other people on here can't see it. The person who runs the site (that's Monica) can see the list of emails, the same way any site's owner can. The only email you ever get is a password reset, and only if you ask for one.

Discord: if you sign on with Discord, your email is whatever your Discord account uses. If you sign on with email too, use that same email, or you will end up with two separate accounts.

Your password is stored scrambled, so nobody can read it, not even the owner.

Messages disappear after a day, and photos and files after 24 hours. DMs are only between you and the other person, but the owner could technically see them in the site's admin dashboard, so don't send anything you wouldn't want an admin to see. *Monica is too lazy and overstimulated to even try to find where to look.

Auto sign-off: if you're away for a while (30 minutes by default) you get signed off so nobody can snoop on your phone. You can change it in the footer after you sign on.

Your profile (name, bio, song, status) is only visible to people who got in with the room code.

Forgot your password? Tap Sign in options, then Reset my password, and type your email. A reset link is emailed to you. If it never shows up, check spam, or ask the owner.

Room code is what keeps strangers out. Ask whoever invited you.
